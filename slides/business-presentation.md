# Sound Realty  
## Pricing Intelligence Tool (Demo)

**Goal:** deliver fast, consistent pricing recommendations to streamline listings and reduce rework.

---

## The business problem

- **Slow pricing cycle**: staff spend too much time estimating value.
- **Inconsistent outcomes**: estimates vary by experience and individual judgment.
- **Bottlenecks during busy periods**: more requests → more delay.

---

## What we built (in plain English)

An internal **Pricing Tool (web UI)** that turns property details into a price recommendation.

- **Simple experience**: enter details → click **Get Prediction** → view price
- **Works with partial information**: supports early-stage valuation when not everything is known
- **Reliable during busy periods**: keeps the experience responsive even when demand spikes
- **Consistent output**: standardizes valuation logic across teams

---

## How teams use it (workflow)

**Where it fits:** listing intake, pricing review, and agent support

1) Agent/analyst enters property details in the UI  
2) Tool returns a recommended price  
3) Team uses it as a baseline for review, comps, and final pricing decision  

**Result:** faster cycles with less back-and-forth and fewer manual steps.

---

## Business value (outcomes you can measure)

- **Cycle time reduction**: quicker pricing recommendations per property
- **Higher consistency**: less variance between staff estimates
- **More throughput**: handle more properties per day without adding headcount
- **Better customer experience**: faster response times to sellers and buyers

---

## Rollout plan & next steps

**Next steps**
- Add reporting dashboards for adoption and pricing outcomes
- Improve UI (input tips, confidence cues, and review checklist)
- Currently the tool supports specific zip codes. We must expand it!
- We need more data to further improve the estimations.
